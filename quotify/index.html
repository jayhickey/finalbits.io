Everything in Quotify 2.0

Smart Photo to Quote

Take or choose any photo, like a book page, sign or screenshot, and Quotify pulls the quote, author and page (Premium).

A Fresh New Design

Cleaner quote rows and Liquid Glass controls. Tap a quote to share it, or swipe and long-press for quick actions.

Choose Your Daily Quotes

Pick which quotes your widgets and reminders show, by any field you've added (Premium).

Home and Lock Screen Widgets

Widgets show quotes from your own library, look right on tinted and clear Home Screens, and can use your own photo (Premium).

Daily Reminders

Daily reminders at the times you choose, as many as you like, now with the author (Premium).

Organize Your Way

Organize quotes with your own fields (Premium), then filter your library by any field and sort by newest, author or quote.

Style and Share

A new Share Quote sheet with Story, Square and Wide images, an accent color, and straight-to-Instagram Stories sharing. Your own photo behind share images (Premium).

Add Quotes Your Way

Record, Paste, Photo or Scan. Share from Safari and other apps, and Safari pages keep their link and title.

Spotlight, Siri and Shortcuts

Find quotes by text or author from Spotlight and Siri, and save new quotes with a Shortcuts action.

Backup and Sync

Private backup and sync across your devices with iCloud, with your sync status in Settings (Premium).

Your Library, Your Data

No account to create. Export your library to CSV or a backup file, and import it back, anytime (Premium).

Quotify 2.0 is ready for iPhone Duo.
