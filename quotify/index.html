Everything in Quotify

Smart Photo to Quote

Take or choose any photo, like a book page, sign or screenshot, and Quotify pulls the quote and author.

A Fresh New Design

Cleaner quote rows and Liquid Glass controls. Tap a quote to share it, or swipe and long-press for quick actions.

Choose Which Quotes Show

Pick which quotes your widgets and reminders show, like just your Favorites or by any field you've added (Premium).

Home and Lock Screen Widgets

Widgets show quotes from your own library and look right on tinted and clear Home Screens.

Daily Reminders

Daily reminders at the times you choose, as many as you like, now with the author (Premium).

Organize Your Way

Mark Favorites, organize quotes with your own fields (Premium), then filter by any field and sort by newest, author or quote.

Save From Other Apps

Share a post, link or text from another app. Safari pages keep their link and title, and Instagram posts save the quote from the image.

Add It, Then Share It

Record, Paste or Photo, or write it yourself. Then share quotes as Story, Square or Wide images, with one tap to Instagram Stories.

Spotlight, Siri and Shortcuts

Find quotes by text or author from Spotlight and Siri, and save new quotes with a Shortcuts action.

Backup and Sync

Private sync across your devices with iCloud, with your sync status in Settings (Premium).

Your Library, Your Data

No account to create. Export your library to CSV or a backup file, and import it back (Premium).
